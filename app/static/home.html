Measure every boundary, road and point in your survey file.

Upload a KML or Shapefile from Google Earth, QGIS or a drone survey. You get the area of every plot and the length of every line, in metres, hectares and acres.

  • Real-world accuracy Shapes are converted from GPS degrees to a metre grid before measuring, then checked against the curved Earth.
  • Messy files welcome Self-crossing boundaries are repaired and flagged. Bad files get a clear reason, not a crash.
  • See it on a map Every shape is drawn on satellite imagery with its measurement written on it.

How it works

  1. 1 Upload

    Drop a .kml file or a zipped Shapefile. We check it's safe and readable.

  2. 2 Convert

    Each shape is moved from GPS degrees onto the local UTM grid, where distances are in metres.

  3. 3 Measure and verify

    Areas and lengths are calculated, then compared with a curved-Earth calculation.

  4. 4 Explore

    See every shape on the map, with totals, attributes and any warnings.
